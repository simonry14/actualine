About the practice

A Ugandan consultancy shaping the built environment with precision and purpose.

Actualine Consults (U) Ltd is a multi-disciplinary consultancy operating in Uganda's built environment — providing professional services across project management, architecture, engineering, quantity surveying, construction contract management, and environmental impact assessments.

We work with developers, institutions, and government bodies to bring complex building projects to life — guiding each phase from feasibility and concept design through construction supervision and handover. Our approach blends rigorous technical practice with deep respect for context: the land, the climate, the community, and the client's vision.

Whether you are planning a private residence, a commercial development, an institutional facility, or a piece of civil infrastructure, we bring the discipline, the people, and the perspective to deliver work that endures.

— Mission

Innovation, made tangible.

To offer innovative, sustainable, dynamic and all-inclusive consultancy services in the built environment — turning ambitious ideas into buildings, infrastructure, and spaces that actually work.

— Vision

Leading East Africa.

To be one of East Africa's leading consultancy firms in the built environment — recognised for technical excellence, integrity, and the lasting quality of the work we put into the world.

— Values

Integrity. Quality. Care.

Every drawing, estimate, and contract bears our name. We hold ourselves to the highest standards of professional ethics, technical accuracy, and accountability to the people who trust us.

How we work

A discipline of care — at every stage of the build.

Great work in the built environment is built on three things: listening carefully, drawing precisely, and following through completely. Our five-stage process is how we put that into practice on every brief.

01

Listen

Brief development, site reading, programme & budget alignment. We invest the time upfront so the design solves the right problem.

— Brief / Feasibility
02

Design

Concepts move through schematic and detail. Architects, engineers, and QSs work as one team — not in silos.

— Concept / Schematic
03

Document

Drawings, specifications, BoQ, contracts. Documentation contractors can build from with confidence.

— Tender / Contract
04

Deliver

On-site supervision, certification, valuation, and contract administration — protecting the client's interest at every stage.

— Construction / Supervision
05

Hand over

Commissioning, snagging, final accounts, defects-period management. A clean transition from build to operation.

— Completion / Defects

Why Actualine

Modern practice. Local roots. Lasting work.

01

Integrated team, not silos.

Architects, engineers, and quantity surveyors work side-by-side from day one. The result: fewer surprises, better coordination, and design decisions that reflect cost and constructability from the start.

02

Built for Uganda — and East Africa.

We understand the local market, the regulatory environment, and the conditions on the ground. Our designs are climate-appropriate, our specifications are buildable, and our contracts are written for how projects actually run here.

03

Sustainability as standard.

Innovative, sustainable, dynamic — these aren't add-ons in our practice; they're the baseline. Passive design, sensible materials, environmental impact thinking, and a long view of operational cost.

04

Senior people on every brief.

You won't be handed off after the pitch. The senior consultants who win the work are the ones who deliver it — present at design reviews, on site, and in the room when decisions are being made.
